# CalVant — AIIA Module User Guide

*Version 1.1 | July 2026*

## 1. Introduction 

The AI Impact Assessment (AI IA) Module in CalVant helps you identify, evaluate, and monitor risks associated with artificial intelligence systems, machine learning models, and automated decision-making algorithms across your organization. It supports structured AI risk evaluations aligned with modern frameworks and governance standards. 

This guide follows the module in the order you'll actually use it. 

## 2. Accessing the AI IA Module

After logging in to CalVant, use the left-hand navigation sidebar to move between modules. Click the AI IA icon in the sidebar to land on your AI Impact Assessment Dashboard. 

## 3. Manual Navigation 

## 3.1 AI Impact Assessment Dashboard 

![Risk Dashboard overview](/Screenshots/ImpactAssessment/AI/1.png) 

The AI Impact Assessment Dashboard is your home base for managing artificial intelligence governance. It gives you an at-a-glance summary of all AI assessments in your organization. 

The screen is organized into three zones: the Overview Zone, the Quick Actions Zone, and the Visual Representation Zone. 

#### Overview Zone

A row of summary tiles showing Total assessments, Approved counts, Completed counts, Pending counts, and Average Complete percentage. 

#### Quick Actions Zone  

Gives one-click access to the most common tasks: 

- View Assessments — Browse all AI IA assessments across the organization. 

![Risk Dashboard overview](/Screenshots/ImpactAssessment/AI/2.png) 

- Plan Assessment — Launch the guided workflow to create a new AI assessment. 

- Manage AI IA — Edit, update, or delete existing AI assessments. 

##### Visual Representation Zone 

- Assessment Status — A breakdown showing assessment approval and completion 

distribution. 

• Assessment Trends — A bar chart displaying monthly distribution of created AI assessments. 

## 3.2 Planning a New AI Assessment 

![Risk Dashboard overview](/Screenshots/ImpactAssessment/AI/3.png) 

To initiate a new AI system evaluation, click Plan Assessment under the Quick Actions Zone on the dashboard to open the Plan AI Assessment window. 

Complete the following core fields to create a new assessment: 

• AI System Name — Enter a descriptive name for the AI model or application (e.g., Customer Support Chatbot). 

• Department — Select the department responsible for or operating the AI system. 

- Risk Owner — Select the designated risk owner from the available personnel list within the chosen department. 

• Assessment Date — Captures the date the assessment is created (defaults to the current date). 

Note: Detailed parameters such as Business Objective, Intended Use, and Foreseeable Misuse will be completed by the assigned Risk Owner before they begin the assessment. Click Create Assessment to save and initialize the record. 

## 3.3 My Assignments & Tracking (User View) 

The My Assignments screen lists all AI assessments assigned to you for review and completion. Access it from your assigned view or dashboard navigation. 

The screen's Overview Zone displays summary counts for Pending items, Completed items, and Total assignments, alongside filter tabs for Pending Action and Completed. 

Each assignment card lists the system name, assignment date, objective summary, status badge (e.g., Action Required), and an interactive Start Assessment button to begin or resume your evaluation workflow. 

## 3.4 Managing and Editing AI Assessments 

![Risk Dashboard overview](/Screenshots/ImpactAssessment/AI/4.png) 

Access the administration view by clicking Manage AI IA from the dashboard Quick Actions Zone to open the Manage AI Assessments window. 

CalVant uses a guided, multi-step process to edit existing records: 

- Step 1 — Assessment Details: Update core identifiers including the AI System Name, 

Department, Business Objective, and Intended Use, then click Next: Assign Risk Owners. 

![Risk Dashboard overview](/Screenshots/ImpactAssessment/AI/5.png) 

• Step 2 — Assign Risk Owners: Select or modify the risk owners associated with the specified department using checkboxes, then click Save Changes to apply updates. 

With this, you've covered the full AI IA Module workflow in CalVant—from planning assessments on the dashboard to tracking assignments and managing system details. Refer back to any section as needed while governing your organization's artificial intelligence initiatives.
