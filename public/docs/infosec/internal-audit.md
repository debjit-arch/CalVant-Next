# **Internal Audit**

As the final step in Calvant, following completion of the Implementation Phase, you need to conduct a complete internal audit of your organisation against ISO/IEC 27001:2022, to verify the effectiveness of implemented controls and identify any nonconformities.

For this, visit the [**"Audit"**](/help-center/audit-module)  module of CalVant. The Audit module has two views — an admin view and an auditor view — and both views are required to complete the internal audit.

## Complete the following tasks:

### 1. Plan the Internal Audit

Set up the internal audit as an audit engagement covering the ISO 27001 requirements and controls.

**A.** From main dashboard in admin view, enter **'Plan Audit'**, then fill in the details - the **'Audit type'**, **'Framework'**, **'Lead Auditor'**, **'Point of Contact'**, and the **'Opening'** and **'Closure Meeting Dates'**.

**Audit → Plan Audit → Audit Details**

##### 1A.1. Open Plan Audit

**A.1.** On the Audit Management dashboard, in the admin view, go to the **'Quick Actions Panel'** and click **'Plan Audit'**.

#### 1B. Assign Controls

**B.** Assign controls to auditors, section by section or by clauses and controls, keeping each auditor outside their own department, as per the independence rule.

**Audit → Plan Audit → Audit Details → Assign Controls**

##### 1B.1. Assign Controls screen

**B.1.** On the **'Assign Controls'** screen (Step 2 of 2), a banner confirms: "Assign auditors to each control. Auditors must be outside the control's department."

##### 1B.2. ISO 27001 control categories

**B.2.** Each category of ISO 27001 — ISMS Core (i.e. ISO 27001 clauses), **Organizational Controls**, **People Controls**, **Physical Controls**, and **Technological Controls** — is listed with its assignment progress (for example, 30/30 assigned). Click the arrow next to a category to expand it and view the individual controls inside.

##### 1B.3. Assign auditor per category

**B.3.** Click the dropdown next to a category and select the auditor to assign to that category's controls. Repeat this for every category.

##### 1B.4. Create Audit

**B.4.** Once every category shows full assignment, click **'Create Audit'** to finish planning the internal audit, or click Back to return to the previous Audit Details step.

### 2. Score each requirement/control against current implementation

Now, auditors can log into their auditor view and review each control to determine what is already in place, what is partially in place, and what is missing.

#### 2.1. Auditor Dashboard view

**1.** When an auditor logs in, the Audit Management dashboard switches to the Auditor Dashboard view, identified by the "Auditor" tag next to the user's name. And a card titled "Controls assigned to [Auditor Name]" confirms: 'Open **'Conduct Audit'** to view and score your assigned controls.'

![Figure 10: c=calvant® ° . _ . a a o : a e lA 7 oe](/Screenshots/Infosec/internal-audit/10.png)

_Figure 10: Audit Management dashboard (auditor view) — Controls assigned card and Quick Actions._

#### 2.2. Open Conduct Audit

**2.** Under **'Quick Actions Panel'**, click Conduct Audit.

#### 2.3. Select the audit engagement

**3.** In the 'Conduct Audit' window, the Filter bar confirms how many audits are assigned (for example, "3 of 3 audits assigned to you"). Each audit is listed as a card showing its status, framework tag, name, number of controls assigned, and a **'My Controls'** progress bar.

Click **'Conduct Audit'** on the relevant audit engagement — the ISO 27001 internal audit created in Task 1.

![Figure 11: © cove oe: ie conduc A Ea e —— ; SE oe](/Screenshots/Infosec/internal-audit/11.png)

_Figure 11: Conduct Audit — select the audit to conduct._

#### 2.4. Score every requirement

**4.** The controls open grouped by category and listed clause by clause and control by control, with the full requirement text shown against each clause. For every requirement:

- **a.** Review the **'Document Evidence'** column, which lists any uploaded files along with the uploader, version, and status (Pending or Approved). Click **'View'** to open a file here.
- **b.** Review the **'Practice Evidence'** column in the same way.
- **c.** Click the **'Document Score'** dropdown and select the applicable ratings: **0 — Non Conformance, 1 — Partial Conformance, or 2 — Conformance**, based on the extent to which the document meets the defined requirements.
- **d.** Click the **'Practice Score'** dropdown and select the same applicable rating using the same scale: **0 — Non Conformance, 1 — Partial Conformance, or 2 — Conformance**, based on the extent to which the practice meets the defined requirements.
- **e.** The 'Total column' updates automatically from the Document Score and Practice Score selected — for example, selecting 2 — Conformance for both evidences produces a Total score of 4.
- **f.** Add any relevant notes in the Document Remarks and Practice Remarks text boxes.

**5.** Once scoring is complete for all assigned requirements, click **'Submit Scores'**.

**Audit → Conduct Audit → Submit Scores**

### 3. Record findings and non-conformities

This step is also carried out in the auditor view.

Review the non-conformities identified for your assigned controls, and for any additional gaps identified while scoring that are not already covered by a logged finding, use **'Add Finding'** to record any additional gaps identified during scoring.

![Figure 14: © convenes oO: G neon dino ncn o](/Screenshots/Infosec/internal-audit/14.png)

_Figure 14: Review and add findings._

**Audit → Review Findings → Add Findings**

### 4. Plan remediation for non-conformities identified

**Audit → Review Findings → Create CAP → Add Task**

#### 4A. Corrective Action Plan screen

**A.** On the 'Corrective Action Plan' screen, each logged finding is listed with its clause number, finding type, requirement text, document and practice remarks, and score.

#### 4B. Root Cause Analysis (5Y)

**B.** Click inside the **'Root Cause Analysis'** (5Y) text box and record the **'Five-whys root cause analysis'** for the finding.

#### 4C. Assigned To

**C.** Click the **'Assigned To'** dropdown and select an owner for the corrective action.

#### 4D. Due Date and Priority

**D.** Click the **'Due Date'** field and select a date, then click the **'Priority'** dropdown and select the priority level: High, Medium or Low.

#### 4E. Status

- **E.** Click the **'Status'** dropdown to update the status of the corrective action — Open, In Progress, or Closed.

#### 4F. Add Task

**F.** Click **'Add Task'** in the Task column to convert the finding into a trackable task. Tasks can be assigned to any user, regardless of their role.

#### 4G. CAP Tracker summary bar

**G.** Once all findings on the screen are updated, the summary bar at the bottom of the screen keeps a running total of 'Major NC', 'Minor NC', 'Open', 'In Progress', 'Closed', and 'Tasks' — this running total serves as your CAP Tracker for the internal audit.

### 5. Internal Audit Report

#### 5A. Audit Reports on the Admin Dashboard

**A.** The **'Audit Reports'** visible on the Admin Dashboard displays the non-compliance percentage across all audits conducted.

**Audit → Audit Reports → View Details → Download Report**

![Figure 16: © canant = ic oo ; : te _ :](/Screenshots/Infosec/internal-audit/16.png)

_Figure 16: Audit Reports._

#### 5B. View Details and Download Report

**B.** Click on **'View Details'** for the **'Internal Audit of ISO 27001'**. This will display the **'Download Report'** option, which allows you to download a PDF copy of the audit report — this is your Internal Audit Report.

![Figure 17: © canant = ic == : - wrsanang he organs are cornet ; — 222 tormatonseourty rk asst . X](/Screenshots/Infosec/internal-audit/17.png)

_Figure 17: Detailed control scores under Audit Reports._

#### 5C. Using the audit report

**C.** The audit report can be used before an audit to identify non-compliance and after the Corrective Action Plan (CAP) is closed to re-check and confirm the updated compliance percentage. Together with the CAP screen from Task 4, this Internal Audit Report and the CAP Tracker form the two records to be carried into Management Review.
