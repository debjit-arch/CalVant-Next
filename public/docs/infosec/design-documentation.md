# **Design & Documentation**

As the next step in Calvant, following completion of the Risk Assessment and Risk Treatment Plan (RA-RTP), you need to design and document the Information Security Management System (ISMS) for your organisation, inclusive of all applicable **Manuals, Policies, Processes, Procedures, Controls, Roles & Responsibilities (R&R)**, and **Templates**, in accordance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022. This includes review of any customization of central documents required by individual divisions.

For this, visit the [**"Policies" module**](/help-center/policy-module)  of Calvant.

## Complete the following tasks:

### 1. Access the Policies Dashboard

**Policies → Policies Dashboard**

**1A.** From the main sidebar, click **'Policies'** to open the Policies Dashboard.

The Policies Dashboard is your home base for the module. It gives an at-a-glance summary of every policy in your organization, along with quick actions to browse, upload, and manage documents. The screen is organized into three zones: the Overview Zone, the Quick Actions Zone, and the Visual Representation Zone.

#### Overview Zone

A row of summary tiles showing the total number of policies, and counts by **'Uploaded'**, **'Pending'**, and **'Archived'**.

#### Quick Actions Zone

Gives one-click access to the most common tasks:

- **'Master List of Documents (MLD)'** - Opens the working register of every policy in your organization.
- **'Upload Documents'** - Opens the policy register, entry point for uploading files.
- **'View Documents'** - Opens the policy register, entry point for browsing existing files.
- **'Archived'** - Opens the Archived Policies page.

#### Visual Representation Zone

- **'Document Status'** — a donut chart showing the proportion of uploaded versus pending documents.
- **'Upload Trends'** — a bar chart of documents uploaded each month, filterable by year.

### 2. Review the Master List of Documents

**Policies → Master List of Documents**

#### 2A. Open the Master List of Documents

**2A.** On the Policies Dashboard, click **'Master List of Documents (MLD)'** under Quick Actions to open the Policies screen — the working register of every policy in your organization.

Here is what you will find on this page:

- **Header stats** - **'Total', 'Uploaded', 'SoA Linked'**, and the count for the active frameworks.
- **Search bar** - Find a policy by name or control ID.
- **Filter chips** - Switch between **'All', 'Uploaded'**, and **'Not Uploaded'**.
- **Table columns** showcase: **Control ID, Policy Name, Related Framework, Type, Control Code, Ownership, Department, CalVant Version**, and **Status**, followed by **Submitted By, Submission Date, Approved By, Review Date, Upload, Remarks, Quality Check**, and **Add Task**.

#### 2B. Sort by Framework

**2B.** Use the dropdown next to the search bar — **'By Framework'** — to reorder the table: groups policies in framework order from ISO 27001.

#### 2C. Locate a policy row

**2C.** Locate the specific policy row using search, filters, or sorting. Scroll right on the table to access the available actions, including **'Upload'**, **'Quality Check'**, **'Add Task'**, and the other document workflow actions.

### 3. Upload, Review, and Approve a Policy Document

**Policies → Master List of Documents → Upload**

#### 3A. Upload the document

**3A.** Scroll right on the relevant policy row and click **'Upload'** to attach the document file for that control.

Once uploaded, the row's **Status** moves from **'To Upload'** to **'To Approve'**, and Calvant records the **Submission Date** automatically.

#### 3B. Post-upload document actions

**3B.** After a document is uploaded, the row's action buttons expand to cover the rest of the approval workflow. The uploaded document can then be checked using the available document actions before it is reviewed by the approver.

1. **The green check icon** would confirm the file has been uploaded.
2. Click **'Quality Check'** to view an AI quality and completeness percentage for the uploaded file.
3. Click **'Re-verify'** to rescan the document after making any changes to it.
4. Click **'History'** to open the version and audit trail for that policy.

### 4. Assign a Documentation Task

**Policies → Master List of Documents → Add Task**

#### 4A. Complete the Create Task panel

**4A.** Click **'Add Task'** on the relevant policy row to open the **'Create Task'** panel. From each field, complete:

- **'Department'** - Select the department responsible for the task.
- **'Assign To'** - Defaults to Auto Assign (Risk Owner); choose a specific person to override it.
- **'Task Description'** - Pre-filled with editable policy name and control reference.
- **'Start Date'** and **'End Date'** - Set the task's working window.
- **'Priority'** - Select **Low, Medium, High**, or **Critical**.
- **'Remarks'** - Optional notes for the assignee.

Click **'Create Task'** to save it. The task then appears against its policy and in the relevant Task module views.

The assigned person will receive the relevant notification via email for the documentation task.

The next set of activities falls upon the **approver** of the documents.

### 5. Review, Approve, or Archive a Policy Document

**Policies → Master List of Documents**

Once the policy document has been uploaded, the approver of the documents can proceed with the review and approval workflow.

#### 5A. Approver review actions

**5A.** The approver can review the uploaded policy document and use the available actions on the relevant policy row.

The approver can:

1. Review the uploaded policy document.
2. Click **'Quality Check'** to view an AI quality and completeness percentage for the uploaded file and use **'Re-verify'** to rescan the document after making any changes to it.
3. Click **'History'** to open the version and audit trail for that policy.

#### 5B. Approve or Archive

**5B.** Once the review is complete, the approver can proceed with the applicable action:

1. The approver would then Click **'Approve'** to move the policy from **'To Approve'** to **'Approved'** once the review is complete.
2. Or would Click **'Archive'** to remove the policy from the active list and move it to Archived Policies (Task 6).

#### 5C. Raise a follow-up task

**5C.** If any further action is required on the policy, the approver can also click **'Add Task'** on the relevant row to create a follow-up task without leaving the list.

The concerned person will receive the relevant notification via email for the task or workflow action, as applicable.

### 6. Archive Superseded Policies

**Policies → Archived**

#### 6A. Open Archived Policies

**6A.** Click **'Archive'** on any policy row, or open **'Archived Policies'** from the **'Archived'** tile on the main dashboard.

Policies removed from the active Master List of Documents are stored here rather than deleted outright, so a record and reason are always kept.

#### 6B. Archived Policies page

**6B.** The Archived Policies page provides the following:

- **Header stats** show **'Total Archived'** and **'Shown'** counts for the current search.
- **Search bar** - Find an archived policy by name, submitter, or department.
- **Table** - Lists **Policy Name, CalVant Version, Submitted By, Department, Uploaded On**, and **Reason (Remarks)**, with **Actions** for you to restore or permanently delete a record.
